PeerChalk

A decentralized, shared whiteboard
for mathematicians & their friends

I had Codex & Claude build this because I don't like the other boards. I'm amazed that with 50 prompts over two days it's already better than the rest.
The end of math... the end of software... that's AI for you, yeay! 😬

No server, peer to peerPeers find each other through OpenDHT, which works much like BitTorrent.
Works in the browserPeers talk over WebRTC, so browsers and the app share the same boards.
FastWritten in C++ and compiled to WebAssembly for the browser.
Behind a firewall?On a strict NAT or a crappy phone connection, turn on a TURN relay.

The app for computers starts faster than this browser version and keeps boards outside the browser's storage. It runs on Linux, Windows and macOS.
