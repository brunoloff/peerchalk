Privacy

PeerChalk is an early test version. It has no account server, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your account, its secret keys, your known people and your boards are stored in this browser, unencrypted, on this device, once per open tab. Clearing the site's data deletes them. Backups you save are encrypted with your passphrase.

Who you share boards with

Board contents, cursors and names go directly to the people you share a board with, over encrypted connections. They can keep what they receive, and removing someone does not delete their copy.

What network services can see

  • The people you connect to can see your IP address.
  • GitHub hosts this site and receives your IP address when you load it.
  • Jami's public OpenDHT proxy (dhtproxy.jami.net) helps devices find each other. It sees your IP address and when you look for peers, but not board contents.
  • Cloudflare's and Google's STUN servers see your IP address when connections are set up.
  • If a TURN relay is used, it sees your IP address and how much data passes through it, but not board contents.

Anyone who has a board link can join that board while an owner is online, so share links only with people you trust.
